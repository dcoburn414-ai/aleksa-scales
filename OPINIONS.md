# OPINIONS

Durable public positions and frameworks. Paraphrase grounded in cited quotes from public posts and long-form (private evidence ledger not in this repo); do not invent private beliefs.

## What clipping is / why it works
- Clipping = take long-form (podcasts, streams, gameplay, libraries) → short-form → distribute on TikTok / Reels / Shorts / X at volume; clippers paid on **CPM** (per 1,000 views).
- Algorithm game is **initial engagement velocity** (watch time, replays, shares, speed of engagement) — not "taste." Distribution is a probability game; one influencer post = one sample; hundreds of clips = hundreds of chances.
- **Manufactured trend spike** (Cluely-style): many accounts post variations of the same brand content in the same window; algorithm reads it like a real trending moment and pushes colder.
- Feed is not random — major creators often have clipper armies behind them (example he cites: Clavicular clip army stats).

## Organic before paid / blended funnel
- Organic short-form CPMs (~$0.10–$2, often ~$0.50–$1 in his examples) beat Meta (~$8–$20 / $10–$20 consumer) on raw reach; organic compounds because posts keep pulling views.
- Paid and organic are one system: organic builds familiarity; paid closes. Use viral organic creatives as Spark/paid ads.
- Influencer one-shots with no performance guarantee lose to performance CPM clip networks.

## Clippable content > clipper count
- Most important campaign input is whether source content is **built to be clipped** (polarizing, story, live reaction) — not headcount of clippers.
- "Clipping doesn't make you interesting. It makes sure more people find out whether you are or not."
- Brand stack that wins: good product + content people naturally talk about + daily short-form distribution systems (his Duel example).

## Formats: slideshows, UGC, faceless
- **Slideshows** currently easiest / lowest CPM format: swipe = engagement, high saves, reader mindset when product appears; product on **one** slide after pain/tension; steal structures that already work; volume over polish; kill on **conversion** not views.
- UGC volume channel: context before commerce; product-first creatives die.
- Faceless/screen-recording often beats creator-led for apps; exception niches needing trust (health/finance/relationships).
- AI UGC: skeptical of "fake real person" approach; wins when leaning into absurd/entertainment AI can do at cost/volume (Cantina exception).

## Ops / agency model
- Path: be a good clipper → proof → first agency deal with 1–2 clippers → systems before scale.
- Client options: in-house (few quality pages) vs mass clipping (tens–hundreds, flood feeds); agencies for scale past ~$10k–$20k/mo spend.
- Clipper retention: wins channels, leaderboards, bonuses/bounties — fight fatigue when most posts flop.
- Pay on results; verify against botted views; review submissions (ops is brutal).
- Public claims: 1B+ views, ~70k–75k creators in network, campaigns for large brands; founded Cliptic (@thecliptic / cliptic.co). Website lists him as Aleksa Ibrahimovic (CEO); X display Aleksa / Aleksa Scales.

## Attention / culture placement
- Attention literacy beats traditional media budgets: if someone at the company will pay ~$15k to place the brand where culture is actually looking (even absurd placements), promote them — they understand attention better than billboard approvers.
- **Brands acting like kids** is how you get seen now; to stay on top, follow whatever is moving right (trend-chasing as distribution, not dignity theater).

## Money / career framing
- **Clipping > YouTube monetisation** "in almost every way": same editing/distribution skill pointed at clipping beats grinding a ~$0.10 YouTube CPM while hoping the page doesn't get banned for something you didn't even do.
- Clipping as broke-friendly business: paid from day 1 while learning **attention conversion**.
- Young advantage = cheap failures; he frames six months at −$100 as best decision.
- Marketing > building for apps in 2026; customers are on social FYPs not "tech X."

## Comment-section leverage beats raw views (X 2026-09-11)
- High views with dead comments lose to lower-view high-comment videos; spike comments on purpose.
- Make content people want to argue with / ask about; seed a controversial comment on page 2; ask a direct question in the caption.
- Comment section is an underused cheap lever.
- Emerging bet: clipping campaigns will hit AI singers / synthetic creators next (watch who moves first).

## Outreach / growth tactics (X 2026-09-12)
- **$1 Whop DM outreach**: send $1 so the recipient gets an email + popup into DMs with the message waiting; spent ~$100, closed 2 deals — frames as most profitable outreach method he has run.

- Attribution / conversion tracking: compare the timeline of views and engagement against when installs come in (indirect tracking).
- Steal/adapt: see what's working in niches close to yours and adapt it for your product.
